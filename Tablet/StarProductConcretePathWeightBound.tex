\begin{helper}
Let \(K\) be a finite field of order \(q\), let \(t\ge2\), and let
\[
  v=(v_0,\ldots,v_{k-1})
\]
be a forward-independent \(k\)-tuple in the star-product digraph
\(D^*(G(t,K))\).  Use the concrete marking rule
\(\noderef{StarProductConcreteMarked}\) with the concrete layer choice
\(\ell(p,r)=\noderef{StarProductLayerChoice}(p,r)\), and use the concrete
unmarked label
\[
  \psi=\noderef{StarProductConcreteUnmarkedPsi}.
\]
If
\[
  \left(1-\frac{1}{32tq}\right)^{w/(t+1)-1}
  <\frac{1}{4q^t}
\]
in the truncated natural-number exponent \(w/(t+1)-1\), then the marked-tuple
signature of \(v\) has binary weight at most \(w\).
\end{helper}
\begin{proof}
Put
\[
  \rho=1-\frac{1}{32tq},\qquad N=2q^t.
\]
Because \(K\) is a finite field of order \(q\), the parameter bounds in
\(\noderef{StarProductPolarityParameterBounds}\) give \(q\ge2\).  Since also
\(t\ge2\), the denominator \(32tq\) is positive and at least \(1\).  Hence
\[
  0\le \rho\le 1.
\]

We first verify the initial bound for the rank-at-most sets from
\(\noderef{StarProductPathRankAtMostSize}\).  For any label
\(\ell\in\{0,\ldots,t\}\), the set \(U_\ell(0)\) is a subset of the full
projective vertex set.  By \(\noderef{PolarityGraphParameters}\), the number
of projective vertices is
\[
  n=\frac{q^{t+1}-1}{q-1}.
\]
The upper estimate in \(\noderef{StarProductPolarityParameterBounds}\) gives
\[
  n\le 2q^t=N.
\]
Therefore \(U_\ell(0)\le N\) for every \(\ell\).

The shrink and nonemptiness hypotheses for the concrete label \(\psi\) are
exactly \(\noderef{StarProductConcreteUnmarkedPathShrink}\) and
\(\noderef{StarProductConcreteUnmarkedPathNonempty}\).  For monotonicity,
\(\noderef{StarProductPathRankAtMostSizeMono}\) says that every
\(U_\ell(i)\) is nonincreasing when one more vertex is appended, so it
applies in particular at all steps not selected as an unmarked step with
label \(\ell\).

It remains to supply the collapse hypothesis required by
\(\noderef{StarProductConcretePathUnmarkedCertificate}\).  The assumed base
inequality says
\[
  \rho^{w/(t+1)-1}<\frac{1}{4q^t}.
\]
Together with \(N\le2q^t\), positivity of \(q^t\), and nonnegativity of the
power of \(\rho\), \(\noderef{StarProductShrinkThresholdCollapse}\) gives
\[
  N\rho^{w/(t+1)-1}<1.
\]
Since \(N\ge0\) and \(0\le\rho\le1\),
\(\noderef{StarProductShrinkCollapseForLaterExponents}\) extends this to
\[
  N\rho^c<1
\]
for every integer \(c\ge w/(t+1)-1\).

All hypotheses of \(\noderef{StarProductConcretePathUnmarkedCertificate}\)
are now verified with the concrete layer choice, the concrete marker, the
concrete unmarked label \(\psi\), \(\rho=1-1/(32tq)\), and \(N=2q^t\).
Applying that certificate gives precisely that the marked-tuple signature of
\(v\) has weight at most \(w\).
\end{proof}
