\begin{lemma}
For every prefix \(p\) and every \(r\ge0\), if \(U_r^p\) is nonempty in
the cardinal sense \(0<|U_r^p|\), then the chosen layer is also nonempty in
the cardinal sense:
\[
  0<|Z_{\ell_r^p}^p|.
\]
\end{lemma}
\begin{proof}
Assume \(0<|U_r^p|\).  By
\(\noderef{StarProductRankAtMostCardLeSuccMulLayerChoice}\),
\[
  |U_r^p|\le (r+1)|Z_{\ell_r^p}^p|.
\]
If \(|Z_{\ell_r^p}^p|=0\), then the right-hand side would be \(0\), forcing
\(|U_r^p|\le0\), contrary to \(0<|U_r^p|\).  Hence
\(|Z_{\ell_r^p}^p|\) is positive.
\end{proof}
