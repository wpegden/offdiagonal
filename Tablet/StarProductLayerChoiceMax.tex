\begin{lemma}
For every prefix \(p\), every \(r\ge0\), and every \(s\le r\), the
choice \(\ell_r^p\) of \(\noderef{StarProductLayerChoice}\) satisfies
\[
  |Z_s^p|\le |Z_{\ell_r^p}^p|.
\]
\end{lemma}
\begin{proof}
By \(\noderef{StarProductLayerChoice}\), the integer \(\ell_r^p\) is chosen
from \(\{0,\ldots,r\}\) so that \(|Z_{\ell_r^p}^p|\) is maximal among all
layer cardinalities indexed by \(\{0,\ldots,r\}\).  Since \(s\le r\), the
index \(s\) belongs to the same interval.  Therefore the maximality property
of the chosen layer gives \(|Z_s^p|\le |Z_{\ell_r^p}^p|\).
\end{proof}
