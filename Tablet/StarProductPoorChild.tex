\begin{definition}
Fix a prefix \(p\), an extension candidate \(x=(a,b)\), and a choice
\(\ell=\ell^p_{r^p(b)}\) of the layer used in the paper.  The child \(x\)
is poor, with respect to \(p\), if
\[
  |N_{G(t,K)}(a)\cap Z_\ell^p|\le |Z_\ell^p|/(8q),
\]
where \(Z_\ell^p\) is the rank layer of \(\noderef{StarProductRankLayer}\).
\end{definition}
