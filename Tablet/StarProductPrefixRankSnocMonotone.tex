\begin{lemma}
Let \(p'=\operatorname{snoc}(p,x)\).  For every projective point \(y\),
\[
  r^p(y)\le r^{p'}(y),
\]
where \(r^p(y)\) is the prefix rank of \(\noderef{StarProductPrefixRank}\).
\end{lemma}
\begin{proof}
By \(\noderef{StarProductPrefixSpanSnocLe}\), the prefix span for \(p\) is a
submodule of the prefix span for \(p'\).  The prefix rank is the finrank of
this submodule by \(\noderef{StarProductPrefixRank}\).  Finrank is monotone
under inclusion of finite-dimensional submodules, so the rank for \(p\) is at
most the rank for \(p'\).
\end{proof}
