\begin{lemma}
For every prefix \(p\) and every \(r\ge0\), if
\(\ell_r^p\) is the layer choice of \(\noderef{StarProductLayerChoice}\),
then, as an inequality of real numbers,
\[
  |Z_{\ell_r^p}^p|\ge \frac{|U_r^p|}{r+1}.
\]
\end{lemma}
\begin{proof}
By \(\noderef{StarProductRankAtMostCardLeSuccMulLayerChoice}\),
\[
  |U_r^p|\le (r+1)|Z_{\ell_r^p}^p|.
\]
The number \(r+1\) is positive.  Dividing both sides of the displayed
inequality by \(r+1\) gives
\[
  \frac{|U_r^p|}{r+1}\le |Z_{\ell_r^p}^p|,
\]
which is the desired real lower bound.
\end{proof}
