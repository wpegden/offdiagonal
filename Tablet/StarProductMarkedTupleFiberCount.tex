\begin{definition}
With \(G\), a marking rule, and the signature convention of
\(\noderef{StarProductMarkedTupleSignature}\) fixed, the star-product
marked-tuple fiber count for a binary string \(z\in\{0,1\}^k\) is the number
of forward independent \(k\)-tuples \(v\) in \(D^*(G)\) whose marked-tuple
signature is exactly \(z\):
\[
  \#\{v:\ v\text{ is forward independent in }D^*(G)
        \text{ and }\operatorname{sig}(v)=z\}.
\]
\end{definition}
