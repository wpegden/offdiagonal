\begin{helper}
Let \(K\) be a finite field of order \(q\), let \(p\) be a prefix of
vertices of \(D^*(G(t,K))\), and fix a projective point \(b\).  Suppose
\[
  r^p(b)=r,\qquad r\le t,
\]
and suppose the geometric-series bound
\[
  \sum_{i=0}^{t-r} q^i\le 2q^{t-r}
\]
holds.  Let \(E\) be a finite set of projective points \(a\) such that each
\(a\in E\) occurs as the first coordinate of a legal forward-independent
extension \((a,b)\) of \(p\) with this fixed second coordinate \(b\).  Then
\[
  |E|\le 2q^{t-r}.
\]
\end{helper}
\begin{proof}
Let \(W=W^p(b)\).  By \(\noderef{StarProductPrefixRank}\), the equality
\(r^p(b)=r\) says that \(\dim_K W=r\).  Let \(W^\perp\) be the orthogonal
complement of \(W\) with respect to the standard dot product on
\(K^{t+1}\).  The standard dot product is nondegenerate on \(K^{t+1}\), so
\[
  \dim_K W^\perp=(t+1)-\dim_K W=t+1-r.
\]

Fix \(a\in E\).  By the defining property of \(E\), there is a legal
extension \(x=(a,b)\) of \(p\).  Applying
\(\noderef{StarProductExtensionOrthogonalToPrefixSpan}\) to this extension
shows that \(\operatorname{rep}(a)\cdot v=0\) for every \(v\in W\).  By
symmetry of the dot product this is exactly the assertion that
\(\operatorname{rep}(a)\in W^\perp\).  Since \(\operatorname{rep}(a)\ne0\),
the point \(a\) is the image of a point of the projective space
\(\mathbb P(W^\perp)\) under the natural inclusion
\(\mathbb P(W^\perp)\to\mathbb P(K^{t+1})\).

Thus every element of \(E\) lifts to \(\mathbb P(W^\perp)\).  The subspace
\(W^\perp\) has dimension \(t+1-r\), and the assumed geometric-series bound
is precisely the numerical hypothesis of
\(\noderef{StarProductFixedBExtensionBound}\).  That node therefore gives
\[
  |E|\le 2q^{t-r}.
\]
\end{proof}
