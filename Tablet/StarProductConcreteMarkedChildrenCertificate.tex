\begin{helper}
Fix the concrete marking rule of \(\noderef{StarProductConcreteMarked}\).
Suppose that for every forward independent prefix of length \(m<k\), the
number of legal popular children is at most \(128tq^t\), and the number of
legal poor children is at most \(10000tq^t\).  If \(A\) is chosen so that
\[
  10128t\le A
\]
and \(h\ge Aq^t\), then every such prefix has at most \(h\) legal marked
children.
\end{helper}
\begin{proof}
Fix a forward independent prefix \(p\).  A legal child marked by the rule
\(\noderef{StarProductConcreteMarked}\) is, by definition of the rule,
either popular in the sense of \(\noderef{StarProductPopularChild}\) or poor
in the sense of \(\noderef{StarProductPoorChild}\).  Therefore the set of
legal marked children injects into the disjoint union of the legal popular
children and the legal poor children.  Its size is at most the sum of the
two sizes.

By the two hypotheses, this sum is at most
\[
  128tq^t+10000tq^t.
\]
The arithmetic helper \(\noderef{StarProductMarkedChildrenBound}\), applied
with the same constants, converts this into
\[
  128tq^t+10000tq^t\le Aq^t.
\]
Finally \(Aq^t\le h\), so the number of legal marked children is at most
\(h\).
\end{proof}
