\begin{helper}
For every integer \(t\ge2\), there is a constant \(C=C(t)>0\) with the
following property.  Let \(K\) be a finite field of order \(q\), and put
\(D^*=D^*(G(t,K))\).  For every \(k\ge1\), if
\[
  C\le q,\qquad Cq(\log q)^2\le k,\qquad k\le eCq^t,
\]
then
\[
  \frac{kq^{t-1}}{4eC}-1 < r(t+1,k).
\]
\end{helper}
\begin{proof}
Fix \(t\ge2\), and take the constant \(C>0\) supplied by
\(\noderef{StarProductForwardIndependentBound}\).  Let \(K,q,k\) satisfy the
displayed hypotheses, and write \(D^*=D^*(G(t,K))\) for the star product
digraph from \(\noderef{StarProductDigraph}\).

By \(\noderef{StarProductDigraphTransitiveFree}\), the digraph \(D^*\) is
\(T_{t+1}\)-free.  Since \(k\ge1\), \(\noderef{DigraphToGraphIndependentSetBound}\)
gives a \(K_{t+1}\)-free graph \(\Gamma\) on the same vertex set as \(D^*\)
such that
\[
  i_k(\Gamma)
  \le
  \left(\frac e k\right)^k \fwi_k(D^*).
\]
Here \(i_k\) and \(\fwi_k\) are the counts formalized in
\(\noderef{SimpleGraphIndependentSetCount}\) and
\(\noderef{ForwardIndependentTupleCount}\), respectively.
The hypotheses \(C\le q\) and \(Cq(\log q)^2\le k\) allow us to apply
\(\noderef{StarProductForwardIndependentBound}\) to \(K\), so
\[
  \fwi_k(D^*)\le (Cq^t)^k.
\]
Combining the two bounds gives
\[
  i_k(\Gamma)\le \left(\frac e k\right)^k (Cq^t)^k. \tag{1}
\]

Set
\[
  p=\frac{k}{eCq^t}.
\]
The positivity of \(C\), \(q\), and \(k\) gives \(p\ge0\), while the hypothesis
\(k\le eCq^t\) gives \(p\le1\).  Multiplying (1) by \(p^k\) yields
\[
  p^k i_k(\Gamma)
  \le
  \left(\frac{k}{eCq^t}\right)^k
  \left(\frac e k\right)^k
  (Cq^t)^k
  =1.
\]
Thus \(\noderef{SamplingKsFreeRamseyBound}\) applies to \(\Gamma\) and gives
\[
  p\,|V(D^*)|-1 < r(t+1,k). \tag{2}
\]
The notation \(r(t+1,k)\) is the Ramsey number from \(\noderef{RamseyNumber}\).

Finally, \(\noderef{StarProductPolarityVertexCountLowerBound}\) gives
\[
  \frac{q^{2t-1}}4\le |V(D^*)|.
\]
Because \(p\ge0\), multiplying this inequality by \(p\) and substituting the
definition of \(p\) gives
\[
  \frac{kq^{t-1}}{4eC}
  =
  \frac{k}{eCq^t}\cdot\frac{q^{2t-1}}4
  \le
  p\,|V(D^*)|.
\]
Combining this with (2) proves
\[
  \frac{kq^{t-1}}{4eC}-1<r(t+1,k),
\]
as claimed.
\end{proof}
