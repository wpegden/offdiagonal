\begin{definition}
For a prefix \(p\) and a rank \(r\), let
\(\operatorname{Pop}^p_r\) be the set of projective points \(b\in Z^p_r\)
of \(\noderef{StarProductRankLayer}\)
which are popular with respect to the layer choice
\(\ell^p_r=\noderef{StarProductLayerChoice}\).  Thus \(b\in
\operatorname{Pop}^p_r\) exactly when \(b\in Z^p_r\) and \(b\) lies in at
least
\[
  |Z^p_{\ell^p_r}|/(16q)
\]
of the subspaces \(W^p(y)\) of \(\noderef{StarProductPrefixSpan}\) with
\(y\in Z^p_{\ell^p_r}\).
\end{definition}
