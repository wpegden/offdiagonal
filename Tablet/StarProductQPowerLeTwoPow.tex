\begin{helper}
Let \(A,C\in\mathbb R\) and let \(t,q,k,w\) be nonnegative integers.  Suppose
\[
  A\ge0,\qquad 2tA\le C\log2,\qquad q\ge2,
\]
\[
  w\le Aq\log q,
  \qquad
  Cq(\log q)^2\le k.
\]
Then
\[
  q^{2tw}\le2^k.
\]
\end{helper}
\begin{proof}
Since \(q\ge2\), \(q>0\) and \(\log q\ge0\).  Taking logarithms, it is enough
to prove
\[
  (2tw)\log q\le k\log2.
\]
The bound \(w\le Aq\log q\), multiplied by the nonnegative factor
\(2t\log q\), gives
\[
  (2tw)\log q\le (2tA)q(\log q)^2.
\]
The scale hypothesis \(2tA\le C\log2\), multiplied by the nonnegative factor
\(q(\log q)^2\), gives
\[
  (2tA)q(\log q)^2\le Cq(\log q)^2\log2.
\]
Finally, \(Cq(\log q)^2\le k\) and \(\log2>0\) imply
\[
  Cq(\log q)^2\log2\le k\log2.
\]
Thus \((2tw)\log q\le k\log2\).  Since \(q>0\), this logarithmic inequality
is equivalent to \(q^{2tw}\le2^k\).
\end{proof}
