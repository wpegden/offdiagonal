\begin{helper}
Let \(K\) be a finite field of order \(q\), and suppose \(t\ge2\).  For
every length \(m<k\) and every legal forward-independent prefix \(p\), the
number of legal children \(x\) satisfying
\[
  \noderef{StarProductPopularChild}
\]
for the concrete layer choice
\(\ell^p_r=\noderef{StarProductLayerChoice}\) is at most
\[
  128tq^t .
\]
\end{helper}
\begin{proof}
Partition legal popular children according to the rank
\[
  r=r^p(b)
\]
of their second coordinate \(b\).  The rank is the dimension of the prefix
span \(W^p(b)\), by \(\noderef{StarProductPrefixRank}\), so it is at most
\(t+1\).  If \(r=t+1\), then
\(\noderef{StarProductFullRankNoForwardIndependentExtension}\) says that no
legal forward-independent extension with second coordinate \(b\) exists.
Thus every legal popular child has rank \(r\le t\).

For each fixed \(r\le t\), \(\noderef{StarProductPopularRankChildrenBound}\)
bounds the number of legal popular children of rank \(r\) by \(64q^t\).
There are \(t+1\) possible ranks \(0,1,\ldots,t\), so the total number of
legal popular children is at most
\[
  (t+1)\cdot 64q^t.
\]
Since \(t\ge2\), we have \(t+1\le2t\).  Therefore
\[
  (t+1)\cdot64q^t\le 128tq^t,
\]
as required.
\end{proof}
