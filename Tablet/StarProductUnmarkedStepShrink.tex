\begin{helper}
Let \(U'\subseteq U\) be finite sets, and let \(G\subseteq U\) be a set
disjoint from \(U'\).  Let \(Z\) be another finite set, and assume
\(t>0\) and \(q>0\).  If
\[
  |G|\ge |Z|/(16q)
  \qquad\text{and}\qquad
  |U|\le2t|Z|,
\]
then
\[
  |U'|\le\left(1-\frac1{32tq}\right)|U|.
\]
\end{helper}
\begin{proof}
Since \(U'\subseteq U\), \(G\subseteq U\), and \(U'\cap G=\emptyset\), the
union \(U'\cup G\) is a subset of \(U\) and its size is
\[
  |U'\cup G|=|U'|+|G|.
\]
Thus
\[
  |U'|+|G|\le |U|,
\]
so
\[
  |U'|\le |U|-|G|. \tag{1}
\]
The lower bound on \(G\) gives
\[
  |U'|\le |U|-|Z|/(16q). \tag{2}
\]
From \(|U|\le2t|Z|\), and since \(t,q>0\), we get
\[
  |U|/(32tq)\le |Z|/(16q). \tag{3}
\]
Combining (2) and (3) yields
\[
  |U'|\le |U|-|U|/(32tq)
  =\left(1-\frac1{32tq}\right)|U|.
\]
\end{proof}
