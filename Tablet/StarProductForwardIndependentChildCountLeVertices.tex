\begin{helper}
Let \(G\) be a loop graph and let \(p\) be any finite prefix of vertices in
the star product digraph \(D^*(G)\).  The number of possible children
obtained by appending one vertex \(x\in V(D^*(G))\) so that the appended
tuple is forward independent is at most \(|V(D^*(G))|\).
\end{helper}
\begin{proof}
By \(\noderef{ProductDigraphVertex}\) and \(\noderef{StarProductDigraph}\),
the possible appended vertices are elements of the vertex set of \(D^*(G)\).
The children under consideration form the subset of those vertices \(x\) for
which appending \(x\) to the fixed prefix \(p\) satisfies
\(\noderef{ForwardIndependentTuple}\).  The map that sends such a child to
its appended vertex \(x\) is simply the inclusion of this subset into
\(V(D^*(G))\), and is injective.  Therefore the number of such children is
at most the total number of vertices of \(D^*(G)\).
\end{proof}
