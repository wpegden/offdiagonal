\begin{helper}
Let \(K\) be a finite field of order \(q\).  For every \(n\ge0\),
\[
  \sum_{i=0}^{n}q^i\le 2q^n .
\]
\end{helper}
\begin{proof}
Since \(K\) is a field, it has at least two elements, so \(q\ge2\).  We prove
the estimate by induction on \(n\).  For \(n=0\) the left hand side is \(1\),
which is at most \(2\).

Assume the claim for \(n\).  Then
\[
  \sum_{i=0}^{n+1}q^i
  =\sum_{i=0}^{n}q^i+q^{n+1}
  \le 2q^n+q^{n+1}
  =(2+q)q^n.
\]
Because \(q\ge2\), we have \(2+q\le2q\).  Hence
\[
  (2+q)q^n\le (2q)q^n=2q^{n+1}.
\]
This completes the induction.
\end{proof}
