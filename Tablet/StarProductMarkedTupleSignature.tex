\begin{definition}
Let \(G\) be a loop graph, and suppose that for every star-product prefix
\(\sigma=(v_0,\ldots,v_{m-1})\) a Boolean marking rule decides whether an
extension \(x\in V(D^*(G))\) is marked.  For a \(k\)-tuple
\[
  v=(v_0,\ldots,v_{k-1})
\]
of vertices of \(D^*(G)\), its marked-tuple signature is the binary string
\(\operatorname{sig}(v)\in\{0,1\}^k\) defined by
\[
  \operatorname{sig}(v)_i=1
\]
exactly when \(v_i\) is not marked as a child of the prefix
\((v_0,\ldots,v_{i-1})\).  Equivalently, \(0\) records a marked extension
and \(1\) records an unmarked extension.
\end{definition}
