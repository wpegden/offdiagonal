\begin{helper}
Let \(A\in\mathbb R\) and let \(t,q,h,\Delta\) be nonnegative integers with
\(t\ge2\) and \(q\ge1\).  Suppose
\[
  h\le Aq^t,\qquad A\le4q^{t-1},\qquad 4q^{2t-1}\le\Delta.
\]
Then \(h\le\Delta\).
\end{helper}
\begin{proof}
Since \(q^t\ge0\), multiplying \(A\le4q^{t-1}\) by \(q^t\) gives
\[
  Aq^t\le4q^{t-1}q^t.
\]
Because \(t\ge2\), we have
\[
  (t-1)+t=2t-1.
\]
Therefore
\[
  4q^{t-1}q^t=4q^{(t-1)+t}=4q^{2t-1}.
\]
Combining this with \(h\le Aq^t\) and \(4q^{2t-1}\le\Delta\) gives
\[
  h\le\Delta.
\]
\end{proof}
