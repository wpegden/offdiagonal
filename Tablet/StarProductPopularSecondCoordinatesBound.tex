\begin{helper}
Let \(K\) be a finite field of order \(q\).  For every prefix \(p\) and
rank \(r\), the set \(\operatorname{Pop}^p_r\) of
\(\noderef{StarProductPopularSecondCoordinates}\) has size at most
\[
  32q^{\ell^p_r},
\]
where \(\ell^p_r=\noderef{StarProductLayerChoice}\).
\end{helper}
\begin{proof}
If \(U^p_r\) of \(\noderef{StarProductRankAtMostSet}\) is empty, then
\(Z^p_r\) of \(\noderef{StarProductRankLayer}\) is empty, and
\(\operatorname{Pop}^p_r\subseteq Z^p_r\) is empty.  The bound is then
trivial.

Assume \(U^p_r\) is nonempty.  By
\(\noderef{StarProductLayerChoicePositive}\), the chosen layer
\(Z^p_{\ell^p_r}\) is nonempty.  Apply the double-counting lemma
\(\noderef{StarProductPopularDoubleCountingBound}\) with
\[
  P=\operatorname{Pop}^p_r,\qquad
  Z=Z^p_{\ell^p_r},\qquad
  W(y)=\operatorname{Pts}(W^p(y)).
\]
For \(b\in P\), the definition
\(\noderef{StarProductPopularSecondCoordinates}\) says exactly that \(b\)
belongs to at least \(|Z|/(16q)\) of the sets \(W(y)\).

For \(y\in Z\), the definition \(\noderef{StarProductRankLayer}\) of
\(Z^p_{\ell^p_r}\) says \(\dim W^p(y)=\ell^p_r\), by
\(\noderef{StarProductPrefixRank}\).
Therefore \(\noderef{StarProductSubspacePointsBound}\) gives
\[
  |W(y)|\le 2q^{\ell^p_r}/q.
\]
The intersection \(W(y)\cap P\) is no larger than \(W(y)\).  Thus the fiber
hypothesis of \(\noderef{StarProductPopularDoubleCountingBound}\) holds, and
that node gives
\[
  |\operatorname{Pop}^p_r|\le 32q^{\ell^p_r}.
\]
\end{proof}
