\begin{helper}
Let \(q,t,e\) be nonnegative integers and let \(N,\rho\in\mathbb R\).  Suppose
\[
  N\le 2q^t,\qquad \rho^e\ge0,\qquad q^t>0,\qquad
  \rho^e<\frac{1}{4q^t}.
\]
Then
\[
  N\rho^e<1.
\]
\end{helper}
\begin{proof}
Since \(\rho^e\ge0\), multiplying \(N\le2q^t\) by \(\rho^e\) gives
\[
  N\rho^e\le 2q^t\rho^e.
\]
The quantity \(2q^t\) is positive, so the strict bound on \(\rho^e\) gives
\[
  2q^t\rho^e
  <2q^t\cdot\frac{1}{4q^t}
  =\frac12.
\]
Thus \(N\rho^e<1/2<1\).
\end{proof}
