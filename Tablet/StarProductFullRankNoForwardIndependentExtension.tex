\begin{helper}
Let \(K\) be a field, let \(p\) be a prefix of vertices of
\(D^*(G(t,K))\), and fix a projective point \(b\).  If
\[
  r^p(b)=t+1,
\]
then there is no vertex \(x=(a,b)\) with second coordinate \(b\) such that
the appended tuple \(\operatorname{snoc}(p,x)\) is forward independent in
\(D^*(G(t,K))\).
\end{helper}
\begin{proof}
Suppose, for contradiction, that such a vertex \(x=(a,b)\) exists and that
the appended tuple is forward independent.  By
\(\noderef{StarProductExtensionOrthogonalToPrefixSpan}\),
\(\operatorname{rep}(a)\cdot v=0\) for every vector \(v\in W^p(b)\).

By \(\noderef{StarProductPrefixRank}\), the hypothesis \(r^p(b)=t+1\) says
that \(W^p(b)\) has the full dimension of \(K^{t+1}\).  Hence
\(W^p(b)=K^{t+1}\).  The preceding orthogonality conclusion therefore says
that \(\operatorname{rep}(a)\cdot v=0\) for every vector
\(v\in K^{t+1}\).  Nondegeneracy of the standard dot product forces
\(\operatorname{rep}(a)=0\), contradicting that a projective representative
is nonzero.  Therefore no such forward-independent extension exists.
\end{proof}
