\begin{definition}
Let \(K\) be a field, let \(t\) be a nonnegative integer, and let
\[
  p=(x_0,\ldots,x_{m-1})
\]
be a prefix of vertices of \(D^*(G(t,K))\), where
\(x_i=(a_i,b_i)\).  For a projective point \(y\), define
\[
  W^p(y)=
  \operatorname{span}_K\{\,b_i:\ a_i y\in E(G(t,K))\,\}
  \subseteq K^{t+1}.
\]
In Lean the projective point \(b_i\) is represented by its canonical
nonzero representative vector, and the span is independent of scaling.
\end{definition}
