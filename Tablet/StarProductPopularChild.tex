\(\ell=\ell^p_{r^p(b)}\) of the rank layer used in the paper.  The child

\]
of the subspaces \(W^p(y)\) with \(y\in Z_\ell^p\), where
\(W^p(y)\) is \(\noderef{StarProductPrefixSpan}\) and
\(Z_\ell^p\) is \(\noderef{StarProductRankLayer}\).
\end{definition}
