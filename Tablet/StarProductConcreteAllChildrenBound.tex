\begin{helper}
Let \(K\) be a finite field of order \(q\), let \(t\ge2\), and let
\(\Delta\) be at least \(4q^{2t-1}\).  Assume the standard projective
cardinality estimates
\[
  |V(G(t,K))|\le 2q^t,\qquad d(G(t,K))\le2q^{t-1}.
\]
Then every forward independent prefix in \(D^*(G(t,K))\), of every length
\(m<k\), has at most \(\Delta\) legal one-step extensions.
\end{helper}
\begin{proof}
Fix a prefix \(p\).  Every legal child is, after forgetting legality, a
vertex of the star-product digraph.  Hence
\(\noderef{StarProductForwardIndependentChildCountLeVertices}\) gives
\[
  \#\{\text{legal children of }p\}\le |V(D^*(G(t,K)))|.
\]
The vertex set of \(D^*(G(t,K))\) is the set of ordered adjacent pairs in
\(G(t,K)\).  Using the projective vertex and degree estimates,
\(\noderef{StarProductPolarityVertexCountBound}\) gives
\[
  |V(D^*(G(t,K)))|\le 4q^{2t-1}.
\]
Since \(\Delta\ge4q^{2t-1}\), the desired bound follows.
\end{proof}
