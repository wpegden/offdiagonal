\begin{helper}
Let \(K\) be a finite field of order \(q\), let \(t\ge2\), and let
\(G=G(t,K)\) be the polarity graph.  Suppose the polarity parameters satisfy
\[
  n=\frac{q^{t+1}-1}{q-1}\le 2q^t,\qquad
  d=\frac{q^t-1}{q-1}\le 2q^{t-1}.
\]
Then the vertex set of the star product digraph satisfies
\[
  |V(D^*(G))|\le 4q^{2t-1}.
\]
\end{helper}
\begin{proof}
By \(\noderef{PolarityGraphParameters}\), the polarity graph is an
\((n,d,\lambda)\)-loop graph with
\[
  n=\frac{q^{t+1}-1}{q-1},\qquad
  d=\frac{q^t-1}{q-1}.
\]
The vertex set of \(D^*(G)\) is the product-digraph vertex type from
\(\noderef{ProductDigraphVertex}\), and \(\noderef{ProductDigraphVertexCard}\)
counts it as
\[
  |V(D^*(G))|=dn.
\]
Using the two assumed upper bounds gives
\[
  dn\le (2q^{t-1})(2q^t)=4q^{(t-1)+t}.
\]
Since \(t\ge2\), the exponent satisfies
\[
  (t-1)+t=2t-1.
\]
Therefore \(|V(D^*(G))|\le4q^{2t-1}\), as claimed.
\end{proof}
