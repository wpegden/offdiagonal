\begin{helper}
Let \(K\) be a finite field of order \(q\), let \(t\ge2\), and let
\(A,k\) be nonnegative integers and \(C\in\mathbb R\).  Put
\[
  w=\left\lfloor A q\log q\right\rfloor,
  \qquad
  \Delta=4q^{2t-1}.
\]
Assume the projective parameter upper bounds
\[
  \frac{q^{t+1}-1}{q-1}\le 2q^t,\qquad
  \frac{q^t-1}{q-1}\le 2q^{t-1}.
\]
Assume also
\[
  10128t\le A,\qquad A\le4q^{t-1},\qquad A\le C,
  \qquad 1\le\log q,
\]
\[
  2tA\le C\log2,\qquad 4A\le C,\qquad Aq^t\ge1,\qquad q\ge4,
\]
\[
  Cq(\log q)^2\le k,
\]
and assume the explicit floor-exponent lower bound
\[
  32tq\log(4q^t)<\frac{w}{t+1}-1,
\]
where \(w/(t+1)-1\) denotes truncated natural-number division and
subtraction.  Then
\[
  \fwi_k(D^*(G(t,K)))\le (Cq^t)^k.
\]
\end{helper}
\begin{proof}
By \(\noderef{StarProductFloorWeightSideConditions}\), applied to the
displayed value of \(w\), the floor inequality and shrink threshold hold:
\[
  w\le Aq\log q
\]
and
\[
  \left(1-\frac{1}{32tq}\right)^{w/(t+1)-1}<\frac{1}{4q^t}.
\]
The hypotheses \(t\ge2\) and \(q\ge4\) supply the auxiliary requirements
\(t\ge1\) and \(q\ge1\) in that node.

Apply \(\noderef{StarProductConcreteTreeCountingBound}\) with this \(w\) and
with \(\Delta=4q^{2t-1}\).  Its hypothesis \(4q^{2t-1}\le\Delta\) is equality,
and its remaining hypotheses are exactly the assumed projective parameter
bounds, \(10128t\le A\), \(A\le4q^{t-1}\), \(A\le C\),
\(1\le\log q\), \(w\le Aq\log q\), \(Cq(\log q)^2\le k\), and the shrink
threshold just obtained.  Hence
\[
  \fwi_k(D^*(G(t,K)))
  \le
  2^k(4q^{2t-1})^w(Aq^t)^{k-w}.        \tag{1}
\]

We now absorb the right-hand side of (1).  First,
\(\noderef{StarProductDeltaPowerLeQPower}\), using \(t\ge1\) and \(q\ge4\),
gives
\[
  (4q^{2t-1})^w\le q^{2tw}.
\]
Second, \(\noderef{StarProductQPowerLeTwoPow}\), using \(A\ge0\),
\(2tA\le C\log2\), \(q\ge2\), \(w\le Aq\log q\), and
\(Cq(\log q)^2\le k\), gives
\[
  q^{2tw}\le2^k.
\]
Finally, the assumptions \(t\ge1\), \(Aq^t\ge0\), \(Aq^t\ge1\), the two
power inequalities above, and \(4A\le C\) are precisely the hypotheses of
\(\noderef{StarProductFinalNumericalAbsorption}\).  Therefore
\[
  2^k(4q^{2t-1})^w(Aq^t)^{k-w}\le(Cq^t)^k.
\]
Combining this with (1) proves the claimed bound
\(\fwi_k(D^*(G(t,K)))\le(Cq^t)^k\).
\end{proof}
