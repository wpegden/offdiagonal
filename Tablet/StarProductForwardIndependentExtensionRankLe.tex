\begin{lemma}
If \(x\) is a forward-independent extension of a prefix \(p\), then
\[
  r^p(x.2)\le t.
\]
\end{lemma}
\begin{proof}
The span \(W^p(x.2)\) is a subspace of the \((t+1)\)-dimensional ambient
space, so its rank is at most \(t+1\).  If the rank were exactly \(t+1\), then
\(\noderef{StarProductFullRankNoForwardIndependentExtension}\) would say that
the appended tuple \(\operatorname{snoc}(p,x)\) is not forward independent,
contrary to the hypothesis.  Hence the rank is not \(t+1\), and the bound
\(r^p(x.2)\le t+1\) leaves only \(r^p(x.2)\le t\).
\end{proof}
