\begin{helper}
Let \(G\) be a loop graph and let \(D^*(G)\) be its star-product digraph.
Fix a marking rule for children of forward independent prefixes, using the
signature convention of \(\noderef{StarProductMarkedTupleSignature}\).  Let
\(\Delta,h\) be nonnegative integers.  Assume that for every
forward independent prefix \(\sigma\) of length \(m<k\):
\[
  \#\{x:\sigma x\text{ is forward independent}\}\le \Delta,
\]
and
\[
  \#\{x:\sigma x\text{ is forward independent and }x\text{ is marked
  from }\sigma\}\le h.
\]
Then, for every binary string \(z\in\{0,1\}^k\), the number of forward
independent \(k\)-tuples in \(D^*(G)\) with marked-tuple signature \(z\) is
at most
\[
  \Delta^{|z|}h^{k-|z|}.
\]
\end{helper}
\begin{proof}
We prove the statement by induction on \(k\).  If \(k=0\), there is only the
empty tuple, its signature is the empty string, and the claimed bound is
\(\Delta^0h^0=1\).

Assume the result for length \(m\), and consider a string
\(z\in\{0,1\}^{m+1}\).  Let \(z'\) be its prefix of length \(m\), and let
\(\epsilon\) be its last bit.  Every forward independent \((m+1)\)-tuple
\[
  v=(v_0,\ldots,v_m)
\]
with signature \(z\) has a forward independent prefix
\[
  p=(v_0,\ldots,v_{m-1})
\]
with signature \(z'\): forward independence restricts to prefixes by
\(\noderef{ForwardIndependentTuple}\), and for each prefix index \(i<m\) the
signature bit is computed from the same earlier prefix and the same vertex
\(v_i\).

Thus the full tuple is determined injectively by its prefix \(p\) and its
last vertex \(v_m\).  For a fixed prefix \(p\), if \(\epsilon=1\), then the
last step is unmarked, and we use only the hypothesis that there are at most
\(\Delta\) legal forward-independent extensions of \(p\).  If
\(\epsilon=0\), then the last step is marked, and the second hypothesis gives
at most \(h\) legal marked extensions of \(p\).  Therefore the number of
tuples with signature \(z\) is at most the number of prefixes with signature
\(z'\), multiplied by \(\Delta\) in the last-bit-one case and by \(h\) in
the last-bit-zero case.

By the induction hypothesis, the number of such prefixes is at most
\[
  \Delta^{|z'|}h^{m-|z'|}.
\]
If \(\epsilon=1\), then
\(\noderef{BinarySequenceWeightSnoc}\) gives \(|z|=|z'|+1\), so multiplying
by \(\Delta\) yields
\[
  \Delta^{|z'|+1}h^{m-|z'|}
  =\Delta^{|z|}h^{(m+1)-|z|}.
\]
If \(\epsilon=0\), then
\(\noderef{BinarySequenceWeightSnoc}\) gives \(|z|=|z'|\), so multiplying by
\(h\) yields
\[
  \Delta^{|z'|}h^{m-|z'|+1}
  =\Delta^{|z|}h^{(m+1)-|z|}.
\]
These two cases complete the induction.
\end{proof}
