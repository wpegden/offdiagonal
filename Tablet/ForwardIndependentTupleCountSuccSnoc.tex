\begin{helper}
Let \(D\) be a finite digraph.  For every \(m\ge0\), the number of forward
independent \((m+1)\)-tuples in \(D\) is equal to the number of pairs
\[
  (p,x),
\]
where \(p\) is an ordered \(m\)-tuple of vertices, \(x\) is one further
vertex, and the tuple obtained by appending \(x\) to \(p\) is forward
independent.
\end{helper}
\begin{proof}
By \(\noderef{ForwardIndependentTupleCount}\), the left hand side counts
ordered maps \(v:\{0,\ldots,m\}\to V(D)\) satisfying
\(\noderef{ForwardIndependentTuple}\).  Such a tuple determines the pair
\[
  \bigl((v_0,\ldots,v_{m-1}),\,v_m\bigr),
\]
and appending \(v_m\) back to the prefix gives the original tuple, so the
resulting appended tuple is forward independent.

Conversely, a pair \((p,x)\) whose appended tuple is forward independent
determines exactly that appended \((m+1)\)-tuple.  These two constructions
are inverse to each other: taking prefix and last entry of an appended tuple
recovers \((p,x)\), while appending the last entry to the prefix of a tuple
recovers the tuple itself.  Thus they give a bijection between the two
finite sets being counted, and the cardinalities are equal.
\end{proof}
