\begin{lemma}
Let \(v\) be a forward-independent path.  For every unmarked step
\(i+1\), the predecessor set selected by
\(\psi=\noderef{StarProductConcreteUnmarkedPsi}\) is nonempty:
\[
  1\le |U_{\psi(i+1)}^{(v_0,\ldots,v_{i-1})}|.
\]
\end{lemma}
\begin{proof}
Let \(p_i=(v_0,\ldots,v_{i-1})\) and \(x=v_i\).  The restriction of the
forward-independent path to the prefix \(p_i\) with \(x\) appended is forward
independent.  Hence \(\noderef{StarProductForwardIndependentExtensionRankLe}\)
gives \(r^{p_i}(x.2)\le t\).  Therefore
\(\noderef{StarProductConcreteUnmarkedPsiApply}\) identifies
\(\psi(i+1)\) with the layer choice \(\ell_{r}^{p_i}\).  Finally,
\(\noderef{StarProductConcreteUnmarkedExtensionNonempty}\) says that
\(U_{\ell_r^{p_i}}^{p_i}\) has real cardinality at least \(1\).  Substituting
the identified value of \(\psi(i+1)\) proves the claim.
\end{proof}
