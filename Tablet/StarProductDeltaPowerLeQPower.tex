\begin{helper}
Let \(t,q,w\) be nonnegative integers with \(t\ge1\) and \(q\ge4\).  Then
\[
  \bigl(4q^{2t-1}\bigr)^w\le q^{2tw}.
\]
\end{helper}
\begin{proof}
Since \(q\ge4\) and \(q^{2t-1}\ge0\),
\[
  4q^{2t-1}\le q q^{2t-1}.
\]
Because \(t\ge1\), \((2t-1)+1=2t\), and hence
\[
  q q^{2t-1}=q^{2t}.
\]
Therefore \(4q^{2t-1}\le q^{2t}\).  Raising both sides to the nonnegative
integer power \(w\) gives
\[
  (4q^{2t-1})^w\le(q^{2t})^w=q^{2tw}.
\]
\end{proof}
