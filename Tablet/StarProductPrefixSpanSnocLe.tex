\begin{lemma}
Let \(p\) be a prefix and let \(p'=\operatorname{snoc}(p,x)\).  For every
projective point \(y\),
\[
  W^p(y)\subseteq W^{p'}(y),
\]
where \(W^p(y)\) is the prefix span of \(\noderef{StarProductPrefixSpan}\).
\end{lemma}
\begin{proof}
By \(\noderef{StarProductPrefixSpan}\), \(W^p(y)\) is spanned by the vectors
\(\operatorname{rep}(p_i.2)\) for indices \(i\) of the old prefix such that
\(p_i.1\) is adjacent to \(y\).  The same old index \(i\) occurs in the
appended prefix \(p'\) as \(i.\operatorname{castSucc}\), and the corresponding
first and second coordinates are unchanged.  Therefore every generator used
for \(W^p(y)\) is also a generator used for \(W^{p'}(y)\).  Taking spans gives
the claimed submodule inclusion.
\end{proof}
