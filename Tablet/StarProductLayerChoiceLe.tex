\begin{lemma}
For every prefix \(p\) and every \(r\ge0\), the chosen index
\(\ell_r^p\) of \(\noderef{StarProductLayerChoice}\) satisfies
\[
  \ell_r^p\le r.
\]
\end{lemma}
\begin{proof}
The definition \(\noderef{StarProductLayerChoice}\) chooses
\(\ell_r^p\) from the finite interval \(\{0,\ldots,r\}\).  Membership in
that interval is exactly the pair of inequalities \(0\le \ell_r^p\) and
\(\ell_r^p\le r\).  The second of these is the claimed bound.
\end{proof}
