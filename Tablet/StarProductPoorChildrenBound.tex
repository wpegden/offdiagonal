\begin{helper}
Let \(K\) be a finite field of order \(q\), and suppose \(t\ge2\).  For
every length \(m<k\) and every legal forward-independent prefix \(p\), the
number of legal children \(x\) satisfying
\[
  \noderef{StarProductPoorChild}
\]
for the concrete layer choice
\(\ell^p_r=\noderef{StarProductLayerChoice}\) is at most
\[
  10000tq^t .
\]
\end{helper}
\begin{proof}
Partition the legal poor children according to the rank
\[
  r=r^p(b)
\]
of their second coordinate \(b\).  By \(\noderef{StarProductPrefixRank}\),
this rank is at most \(t+1\).  If \(r=t+1\), then
\(\noderef{StarProductFullRankNoForwardIndependentExtension}\) says that no
legal forward-independent extension with second coordinate \(b\) exists.
Thus every legal poor child has rank \(r\le t\).

For each fixed \(r\le t\),
\(\noderef{StarProductPoorRankChildrenBound}\) bounds the number of legal
poor children of rank \(r\) by \(5000q^t\).  There are \(t+1\) possible
ranks \(0,1,\ldots,t\), so the total number of legal poor children is at
most
\[
  (t+1)\cdot5000q^t.
\]
Since \(t\ge2\), we have \(t+1\le2t\).  Therefore
\[
  (t+1)\cdot5000q^t\le10000tq^t,
\]
as required.
\end{proof}
