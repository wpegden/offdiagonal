\begin{lemma}
Let \(p'=\operatorname{snoc}(p,x)\).  For every integer \(l\),
\[
  U_l^{p'}\subseteq U_l^p,
\]
where \(U_l^p=\{y:r^p(y)\le l\}\) is the set
\(\noderef{StarProductRankAtMostSet}\).
\end{lemma}
\begin{proof}
Take \(y\in U_l^{p'}\).  Then \(r^{p'}(y)\le l\) by
\(\noderef{StarProductRankAtMostSet}\).  By
\(\noderef{StarProductPrefixSpanSnocLe}\), the span defining \(r^p(y)\) is a
subspace of the span defining \(r^{p'}(y)\).  Since finrank is monotone under
subspace inclusion, \(r^p(y)\le r^{p'}(y)\).
Hence \(r^p(y)\le l\), so \(y\in U_l^p\).
\end{proof}
