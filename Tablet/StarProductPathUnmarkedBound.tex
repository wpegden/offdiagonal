\begin{helper}
Fix nonnegative integers \(t,m,w\), real numbers \(N,\rho\), and for each
\(\ell\in\{0,\ldots,t\}\) a sequence \(U_\ell(0),\ldots,U_\ell(m)\).  Suppose
some indices \(i\in\{1,\ldots,m\}\) are called unmarked and each unmarked
index has a label \(\psi(i)\in\{0,\ldots,t\}\).  Assume \(\rho\ge0\),
\[
  U_\ell(0)\le N\qquad\text{for every }\ell,
\]
and for every \(0\le i<m\):
if \(i+1\) is unmarked, then
\[
  U_{\psi(i+1)}(i+1)\le \rho\,U_{\psi(i+1)}(i);
\]
while for every label \(\ell\), if \(i+1\) is not an unmarked index with
label \(\ell\), then
\[
  U_\ell(i+1)\le U_\ell(i).
\]
Assume also that every unmarked index has nonempty preceding set,
\[
  1\le U_{\psi(i+1)}(i)
  \qquad(0\le i<m),
\]
and that for every nonnegative integer \(c\) satisfying
\[
  c\ge
  \max\left\{\left\lfloor\frac{w}{t+1}\right\rfloor-1,0\right\},
\]
\[
  N\rho^c<1.
\]
Then the number of unmarked indices in \(\{1,\ldots,m\}\) is at most \(w\).
\end{helper}
\begin{proof}
Let \(S\) be the set of unmarked indices.  Suppose, for contradiction, that
\(|S|>w\).  Coloring each element of \(S\) by its label \(\psi(i)\) gives
\(t+1\) color classes.  Since
\[
  (t+1)\left\lfloor\frac{w}{t+1}\right\rfloor\le w<|S|,
\]
the finite pigeonhole principle gives a label \(\ell\) for which the color
class
\[
  S_\ell=\{i\in S:\psi(i)=\ell\}
\]
has more than \(\lfloor w/(t+1)\rfloor\) elements.

Let \(i^*\) be the largest element of \(S_\ell\), and put
\(r=\lfloor w/(t+1)\rfloor\).  Since \(|S_\ell|>r\), deleting \(i^*\)
leaves at least \(r\) elements.  Every other element of \(S_\ell\) lies in
\(\{1,\ldots,i^*-1\}\), so in particular
\[
  |S_\ell\cap\{1,\ldots,i^*-1\}|
    \ge
    \max\left\{\left\lfloor\frac{w}{t+1}\right\rfloor-1,0\right\}. \tag{1}
\]
Apply \(\noderef{StarProductIteratedShrink}\) to the single sequence
\(U_\ell\), with selected indices those unmarked indices whose label is
\(\ell\).  The hypotheses above say exactly that selected steps shrink by
the factor \(\rho\) and all other steps are nonincreasing.  Hence
\[
  U_\ell(i^*-1)
  \le
  N\rho^{|S_\ell\cap\{1,\ldots,i^*-1\}|}.
\]
By (1) and the assumed collapse bound, the right-hand side is \(<1\).  Thus
\[
  U_\ell(i^*-1)<1.
\]
On the other hand, \(i^*\) is unmarked and has label \(\ell\), so the
nonemptiness hypothesis gives
\[
  1\le U_{\psi(i^*)}(i^*-1)=U_\ell(i^*-1),
\]
a contradiction.  Therefore \(|S|\le w\).
\end{proof}
