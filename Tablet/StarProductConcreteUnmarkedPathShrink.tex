\begin{lemma}
Let \(v\) be a forward-independent path and let
\(\psi=\noderef{StarProductConcreteUnmarkedPsi}\).  For every unmarked step
\(i+1\),
\[
  |U_{\psi(i+1)}^{(v_0,\ldots,v_i)}|
  \le
  \left(1-\frac{1}{32tq}\right)
  |U_{\psi(i+1)}^{(v_0,\ldots,v_{i-1})}|.
\]
\end{lemma}
\begin{proof}
Let \(p_i=(v_0,\ldots,v_{i-1})\) and \(x=v_i\).  The prefix \(p_i\) with
\(x\) appended is forward independent because it is an initial segment of the
forward-independent path.  The signature bit for step \(i\) is true precisely
when the concrete marker \(\noderef{StarProductConcreteMarked}\) is false for
the child \(x\); this is the definition
\(\noderef{StarProductMarkedTupleSignature}\).

By \(\noderef{StarProductForwardIndependentExtensionRankLe}\), the rank
\(r^{p_i}(x.2)\) is at most \(t\).  Thus
\(\noderef{StarProductConcreteUnmarkedPsiApply}\) identifies
\(\psi(i+1)\) with the chosen layer \(\ell_r^{p_i}\).  Applying
\(\noderef{StarProductConcreteUnmarkedExtensionShrink}\) to the
forward-independent unmarked extension \(p_i\mapsto p_i\cup\{x\}\) gives the
shrink inequality for \(U_{\ell_r^{p_i}}\).  Replacing
\(\ell_r^{p_i}\) by the identified value \(\psi(i+1)\) is exactly the displayed
path-level inequality.
\end{proof}
