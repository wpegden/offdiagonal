\begin{helper}
Let \(K\) be a finite field of order \(q\).  Let \(W^\perp\) be a subspace
of \(K^{t+1}\) of dimension \(t+1-r\), and let \(E\) be a finite set of
projective points in \(\mathbb P(K^{t+1})\).  Suppose every point of \(E\)
is the image of a point of \(\mathbb P(W^\perp)\) under the natural inclusion
\(\mathbb P(W^\perp)\to\mathbb P(K^{t+1})\), and suppose
\[
  \sum_{i=0}^{t-r} q^i\le 2q^{t-r}.
\]
Then
\[
  |E|\le 2q^{t-r}.
\]
\end{helper}
\begin{proof}
Choose, for each \(a\in E\), one projective point
\(\widetilde a\in\mathbb P(W^\perp)\) mapping to \(a\).  The projectivized
linear inclusion \(W^\perp\hookrightarrow K^{t+1}\) is injective on
projective points, so if \(\widetilde a=\widetilde a'\), then their images
are equal and hence \(a=a'\).  Thus this choice gives an injection
\[
  E\hookrightarrow \mathbb P(W^\perp).
\]
Therefore
\[
  |E|\le |\mathbb P(W^\perp)|.
\]
Since \(W^\perp\) has dimension \(t+1-r\) over the field of order \(q\), the
standard finite projective-space count gives
\[
  |\mathbb P(W^\perp)|=\sum_{i=0}^{t-r}q^i.
\]
The assumed geometric-series bound then gives
\[
  |E|\le \sum_{i=0}^{t-r}q^i\le 2q^{t-r},
\]
as required.
\end{proof}
