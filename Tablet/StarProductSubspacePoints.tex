\begin{definition}
For a prefix \(p\) and a projective point \(y\), let
\(\operatorname{Pts}(W^p(y))\) be the finite set of projective points \(b\)
whose chosen representative lies in the prefix span
\(W^p(y)\) of \(\noderef{StarProductPrefixSpan}\):
\[
  \operatorname{Pts}(W^p(y))
  =\{b:\operatorname{rep}(b)\in W^p(y)\}.
\]
\end{definition}
