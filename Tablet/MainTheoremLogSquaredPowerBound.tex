\begin{helper}
There is a real number \(Q_0\) such that every integer \(q\ge Q_0\) satisfies
\[
  4(\log q)^2\le e q.
\]
\end{helper}
\begin{proof}
The standard asymptotic estimate \((\log x)^2=o(x)\) says that, for every
positive constant \(\epsilon\), all sufficiently large real \(x\) satisfy
\[
  |(\log x)^2|\le \epsilon |x|.
\]
Apply this with \(\epsilon=e/4\).  For positive integers \(q\) beyond the
resulting threshold, both \(q\) and \((\log q)^2\) are nonnegative, so the
absolute values may be removed and
\[
  (\log q)^2\le \frac e4 q.
\]
Multiplying by \(4\) gives the stated bound.
\end{proof}
