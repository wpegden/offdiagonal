\begin{helper}
Let \(G\) be a loop graph and let
\[
  v_i=(a_i,b_i)\in V(D^*(G)),\qquad i\in\{1,\ldots,k\},
\]
be a \(k\)-tuple of vertices of the star product digraph.  Then this
\(k\)-tuple is forward independent in \(D^*(G)\) if and only if the
coordinate \(2k\)-tuple \((a_1,b_1,\ldots,a_k,b_k)\) is \(D^*\)-consistent:
\[
  a_i b_i\in E(G)\quad\text{for every }i,
\]
and, whenever \(i<j\) and \(a_i b_j\in E(G)\), one has
\[
  a_j b_i\in E(G).
\]
\end{helper}
\begin{proof}
By \(\noderef{ProductDigraphVertex}\), the assertion
\((a_i,b_i)\in V(D^*(G))\) already means \(a_i b_i\in E(G)\) for every
\(i\).  By \(\noderef{StarProductDigraph}\), there is an arc from
\((a_i,b_i)\) to \((a_j,b_j)\) exactly when
\[
  a_i b_j\in E(G)\quad\text{and}\quad a_j b_i\notin E(G).
\]

Assume first that the tuple is forward independent in the sense of
\(\noderef{ForwardIndependentTuple}\).  Fix \(i<j\) and suppose
\(a_i b_j\in E(G)\).  If \(a_j b_i\notin E(G)\), then the displayed
criterion from \(\noderef{StarProductDigraph}\) gives an arc from the
\(i\)-th vertex to the \(j\)-th vertex, contradicting forward independence.
Hence \(a_j b_i\in E(G)\).  Together with the vertex conditions above, this
is exactly \(D^*\)-consistency as defined in
\(\noderef{StarProductConsistentTuple}\).

Conversely, suppose the coordinate tuple is \(D^*\)-consistent.  If
\(i<j\) and there were an arc from \((a_i,b_i)\) to \((a_j,b_j)\), then
\(\noderef{StarProductDigraph}\) would give both \(a_i b_j\in E(G)\) and
\(a_j b_i\notin E(G)\).  The consistency condition from
\(\noderef{StarProductConsistentTuple}\) applied to \(i<j\) and
\(a_i b_j\in E(G)\) gives \(a_j b_i\in E(G)\), a contradiction.  Thus no
forward arc exists, so the tuple is forward independent.
\end{proof}
